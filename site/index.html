The protocol

Settlement you can verify, not just trust.

Smart contracts cannot see the world, and price feeds cover only one kind of question. Mimir settles any question that names a public source: a match result, a price at a set time, whether an event happened by a date.

01
Fixed terms. The question, both sides, the deadline and the source are written on chain at creation and cannot change.
02
Sealed evidence. Each proposal carries the sha256 of everything the decision rested on: source text, price readings, model and reasoning.
03
Challengeable by design. A 24-hour dispute window, an independent audit agent and a multisig arbiter stand between a proposal and a payout.

How settlement works

Four steps from question to payout.

Market lifecycle →
  1. 01

    Ask

    A market is created with its wording, two sides, a deadline and the public source that will settle it. Staking closes 60 seconds before the deadline.

    createMarket · createClaim
  2. 02

    Propose

    After the deadline the oracle fetches fresh evidence and proposes an outcome, an eligibility cutoff and the hash of its evidence bundle.

    resolveWithCutoff
  3. 03

    Challenge

    For 24 hours a participant, the audit agent or the arbiter can dispute with a 2 USDC bond. Only the arbiter, a 2-of-3 Safe, can rule.

    dispute · resolveDisputeWithCutoff
  4. 04

    Settle

    The result becomes final and winners are paid in USDC. Draws and unresolvable questions refund. After seven days anyone can force a refund.

    finalize · claim · refundExpired

Trust model

A wrong answer has to get past four checks.

Disputes and arbitration →
01

Evidence on record

The evidence bundle's hash is committed on chain with the proposal. What the oracle saw, and why, can be reviewed.

02

Bonded disputes

Any participant can challenge with 2 USDC. A correct challenge is refunded; a wrong one is forfeited.

03

Independent audit agent

A second AI on another model and host re-checks every proposal blind, and disputes at 90%+ confidence.

04

Multisig arbiter

A 2-of-3 Safe rules on disputes. The oracle can only propose; it can never rule or move funds.
