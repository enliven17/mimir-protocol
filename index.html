How it works

From question to payout,
all on chain

  1. AskA question goes on chain with its exact wording, two sides, a deadline and the public source that will settle it. Nobody can change them later.
  2. ProposeAfter the deadline the oracle fetches fresh evidence and proposes an outcome, a confidence score and the hash of its full reasoning.
  3. ChallengeFor 24 hours anyone involved, the audit agent or the arbiter can dispute with a 2 USDC bond. The arbiter rules.
  4. SettleThe result is final and winners are paid in USDC. Unclear questions refund everyone, and a 7-day escape hatch means funds never get stuck.
Trust

AI can be wrong.
Mimir is built to catch it.

One mistaken answer has to get past four independent checks before any money moves.

01

Evidence on record

Every proposal carries a sha256 hash of its sources and reasoning. What the oracle saw, and why it decided, can be checked by anyone.

02

Bonded disputes

Disagree? Post 2 USDC within the window. Right, and you get it back with the result corrected. Wrong, and the bond is forfeited.

03

Independent audit agent

A second AI, on a different model and with its own keys, re-checks every result from scratch without seeing the oracle's answer, and disputes on its own.

04

Multisig arbiter

A 2-of-3 Safe makes the final call on any dispute. The oracle can only propose; it can never rule or move funds.

No sniping known results

If the outcome is already known before the deadline, say once a match has started, the oracle sets an eligibility cutoff. Stakes placed after it are simply refunded, and earlier stakes settle as normal.

The details

How the oracle decides
  1. Deterministic rules first. A market with only one side staked is refunded without asking a model. Known feeds, such as final sports scores, are read directly.
  2. Fresh evidence. The oracle fetches the resolution source at settlement time, plus two independent price feeds for price questions. Source text is fenced as untrusted input, so instructions hidden in a page can't steer the model.
  3. A settlement-grade model answers the original question as written, with reasoning. Creator-supplied shortcuts can't override it.
  4. Confidence decides the action. 80% or more settles; 60–79% settles marked contested; below 60% refunds. Missing or ambiguous evidence is retried after 10 minutes instead of guessed.
  5. The evidence is sealed. The full bundle (source, answer, cutoff, reasoning, model) is hashed and the sha256 goes on chain with the proposal.

The oracle runs every two minutes. One key signs on every chain, and it can only propose.

The eligibility cutoff

With each proposal the oracle sets a cutoff: the start time of a scheduled event, the moment a "by date" event happened, or a price's measurement time. Only deposits strictly before the cutoff compete. Later deposits are refunded at face value, without profit, and never fund someone else's prize. The contracts compute this from their own on-chain deposit history. The audit agent and the arbiter can correct the cutoff just like the outcome.

The audit agent
  • Reads proposals and the original question, sides and source straight from the chain. It never reuses the oracle's cache or verdict.
  • Fetches fresh evidence and decides both the answer and the cutoff on its own, on a different model.
  • Disputes only on evidence-backed disagreement at 90% confidence or more.
  • Can only open the normal bonded dispute: it cannot rule, veto or move funds. The owner Safe registers it with setAuditAgent, and it must differ from every privileged role.

Pause stops new stakes, proposals and winning payouts, but refunds and withdrawals keep working. Caps are 500 USDC per market and 50 USDC per account at launch. The oracle can be revoked instantly.

Fees
  • Entry fee: 0.5% of each stake, hard-capped at 1%, to the treasury Safe.
  • Copy trades: 1% of net profit to the copied trader and 1% to Mimir, on wins only.
  • Wrong dispute: the 2 USDC bond goes to the treasury.
  • Winnings: no fee.
Security status
  • Open source contracts, verified on Sourcify (exact match).
  • Internal security reviews before launch: two rounds in October 2026 and a pre-mainnet audit pass in November 2026, with every finding fixed.
  • 288 Foundry tests, including invariant suites (escrow solvency, payouts never exceed the pot) and fork tests against real Base USDC and a real Safe.
  • The full lifecycle, including an audit-agent dispute and an arbiter ruling, was rehearsed on Base Sepolia.
  • No independent external audit yet. Stake caps stay low until there is one.
  • Arc mainnet runs an earlier version of the contracts (MimirV3 0x48fa…7fb0, MimirPool 0xc315…b1b0) and will move to the current version later.
