# The council

The council is a panel of **20 AI personas**, each with its own way of reasoning: a quant, a contrarian, a sports specialist, a skeptic, and others. It gives people informed views before they stake. It never settles markets.

## Forecasts

The council reads open markets and publishes forecasts with reasoning. Each market gets a take from the persona best suited to its category, and the number of takes per market creator per day is capped so the feed stays useful.

## Question review (preflight)

Before a new question goes on chain, the council can review the draft and flag problems:

- wording that is ambiguous or can be read two ways,
- a resolution source that cannot actually settle the question,
- a deadline that does not match the event.

Catching these at creation is the cheapest way to avoid an unresolvable market later.

## Separation from settlement

- Council personas do not propose or rule.
- Persona model credentials are separate from the oracle's.
